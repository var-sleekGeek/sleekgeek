Ongoing maintenance and reliable hosting so your site stays secure, fast, and up-to-date.

What this service includes

This service bundles predictable, professional care for your site: regular software and dependency updates, security patches, daily/weekly backups, uptime monitoring, performance tuning, content updates on request, and managed hosting with optimized caching and TLS. Options include automated deployments, staging environments, and a clear incident response plan for critical outages.

Why it matters

Websites require ongoing attention. Unpatched plugins or outdated libraries create security vulnerabilities; performance can degrade if not monitored; and downtime harms both SEO and customer trust. A managed maintenance and hosting plan reduces risk, keeps the site performant and secure, and frees your team to focus on product and content rather than infrastructure.

Why Sleek Geek is the right choice

Sleek Geek offers a pragmatic, transparent approach to maintenance: clear scopes, predictable billing, and prioritized updates that preserve stability. The team configures hosting for performance and security, runs routine audits, and provides timely support when issues arise.
