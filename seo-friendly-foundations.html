SEO‑Friendly Foundations

Back to Services

Technical and on-page SEO that gives your site the best chance to be discovered and ranked.

What this service includes

SEO‑Friendly Foundations covers the technical building blocks search engines rely on: semantic HTML and heading structure, correct use of meta titles and descriptions, canonicalization, crawl-friendly sitemaps and robots directives, fast page performance (image optimization, caching, critical CSS), mobile-first responsive markup, and structured data (JSON-LD) for rich results. On-page work includes meaningful content structure and basic keyword targeting aligned with your audience.

Why it matters

Without proper foundations, even great content can remain invisible. Technical SEO ensures search engines can crawl and index pages efficiently, and that performance and mobile experience meet modern ranking signals. This translates into more organic traffic, higher visibility for keywords that matter to your business, and better return on content and marketing investment.

Why Sleek Geek is the right choice

Sleek Geek builds SEO into the project from day one, not as an afterthought. The team balances semantic, accessible markup with performance-first engineering so pages load fast and communicate clearly to search engines and users alike. Deliverables include a technical SEO checklist, structured data implementation where appropriate, and actionable recommendations for ongoing content and link-building strategy.
