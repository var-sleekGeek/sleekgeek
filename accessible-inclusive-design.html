Accessible & Inclusive Design

Back to Services

Design and build digital experiences that everyone can use — regardless of ability, device, or context.

What this service includes

Accessible & Inclusive Design is a combination of technical implementation and human-centered design. Services include semantic HTML structure, meaningful alt text and labels, keyboard navigation, ARIA where appropriate, color contrast and typography adjustments, form accessibility, and assistive-technology testing (screen readers, keyboard-only flows). The service also includes an accessibility audit against WCAG guidelines, prioritized remediation recommendations, and documentation so your team can maintain accessibility over time.

Why it matters

Accessibility is not only the right thing to do — it expands your audience, improves usability for everyone, and reduces legal and reputation risk. Accessible sites often have better performance and SEO, lower bounce rates, and higher conversion for users on slow connections or mobile devices. Making a product inclusive creates better experiences for more people and strengthens trust in your brand.

Why Sleek Geek is the right choice

Sleek Geek brings practical accessibility into every project rather than treating it as an afterthought. The team combines design sensitivity with engineering discipline: accessible patterns are implemented in clean, maintainable HTML/CSS and validated with automated checks and manual assistive-technology testing. Deliverables include an accessibility report, prioritized fixes, and developer-friendly documentation so accessibility is preserved as your product evolves.
