Security Enhancements

Back to Services

Practical security improvements to protect your site, your users, and your data.

What this service includes

Security Enhancements covers a range of hardening and protection measures: enforcing HTTPS and HSTS, secure cookie flags, Content Security Policy (CSP), secure headers, input validation and sanitization, authentication best practices (password policies, multi-factor options), rate-limiting and brute-force protections, vulnerability scanning, and optional penetration testing. The service also provides an incident response plan and guidance on backups and disaster recovery.

Why it matters

Security incidents cost time, money, and trust. Weak configurations, unpatched components, and missing protections make sites easy targets. Proactively addressing security reduces the chance of data breaches, downtime, SEO penalties, and reputational damage — and it demonstrates to users that their privacy and data are taken seriously.

Why Sleek Geek is the right choice

Sleek Geek prioritizes secure defaults and pragmatic mitigations — the focus is on lowering risk without adding unnecessary complexity. The team performs meaningful security reviews, applies best-practice configuration, and documents choices so you can maintain a secure posture as the product grows. When required, Sleek Geek coordinates with hosting providers and external auditors to deliver a complete, tested security solution.

To discuss a security review or improve your current setup, contact Sleek Geek.
